About us

Rich Diamond Capital is an international family office and asset management institution registered in the Cayman Islands, founded in 2015 by Canadian-Chinese entrepreneur Richard Lin. Upholding the mission of "Empower Future Technology, Connect Global Capital," the firm focuses on long-term structural value investments in global emerging industries and cutting-edge technology.

Adopts a distinctive dual-driving structure:

  • Family Wealth Management Center: Wholly owned by the founding family, it manages proprietary family capital and conducts global asset allocation with prudence for intergenerational wealth.
  • Rich Diamond Asset Management: A licensed asset management platform under the Group. Serving as General Partner (GP), it professionally manages capital from Middle Eastern sovereign wealth funds, institutional funds across the Asia-Pacific region and special-purpose funds focusing on hard technology.
Family Legacy

Rich Diamond Capital is wholly owned by the founding family with no external shareholders and no public fundraising activities. This ownership structure endows us with three distinctive capital advantages:

No Redemption Pressure

Aligned with the lifecycle of family wealth, our family capital never forces divestment of core assets at low valuations amid market panic or short-term market volatility.

No Leverage Dependence

The overall leverage ratio of our investment portfolio is consistently kept below 15% in the long run. Core tech assets are held with full cash or minimal leverage, ensuring resilience amid extreme market conditions.

No Short-term Performance Assessment

We evaluate investment teams with a minimum five-year assessment cycle. Core evaluation metrics focus on DPI and strategic value creation, rather than annual IRR rankings.

Our Core Belief: Genuine technological transformation demands patient capital spanning over a decade. We safeguard every investment with the same prudence as preserving family wealth, embracing intergenerational thinking over quarterly thinking.

Investment Strategy

Deep Value Discovery via Industrial Capital Vision

01 Strategic Industrial Chain Positioning

Rather than pursuing maximum financial returns from individual projects, we adopt industry mapping to make targeted investments at pivotal industrial chain nodes and build synergistic investment portfolios.

02 Long-term Partnership with Leading Enterprises

We select only one or two top-tier enterprises within each niche track, inject capital with substantial ticket sizes, secure board or observer seats, and deeply engage in their 3-5 year strategic planning and resource integration.

03 Industrial Ecosystem Synergy

Leveraging Rich Diamond Capital's global industrial network, we actively facilitate technological collaboration, supply chain integration and cross-border market expansion among portfolio companies, turning the investment portfolio into an integrated industrial community.

Investment Platforms

Family Direct Investment Account

It manages core family wealth, holding global listed and unlisted tech assets directly via all-cash or ultra-low-leverage strategies. Core holdings feature an investment horizon of 7 to 10 years, accounting for around 40% of total AUM.

Middle East Technology Allocation Fund

Co-launched with a platform under Abu Dhabi sovereign wealth fund, this fund totals $1.2 billion. It focuses on global energy technology, AI infrastructure and advanced manufacturing projects that support economic diversification in Middle East (Vision 2030).

Asia-Pacific Hard Technology Special Fund

With a scale of $800 million, it focuses on semiconductor equipment, advanced packaging and industrial automation across Greater China, Japan, South Korea and Southeast Asia. Its LPs include Asia-Pacific sovereign funds, university endowments and family office alliances.
